SERVICES AGREEMENT – Investor pitch deck for Polar Analytics

1. Parties
Client: Polar Analytics GmbH, reg. no. HRB 228734
Supplier: Kvist Creative AS, org. no. 927 330 214

2. Scope
This agreement covers the design of an investor pitch deck and a matching one-page summary. The work includes:
- Pitch deck of up to 20 slides
- Editable PowerPoint template
- One-page summary (PDF)
- Icon set of 15 icons
This agreement is based on the quote dated 02/11/2026.

3. Price
The Client shall pay a fixed fee of NOK 45,000 excl. VAT.

4. Revisions
Three rounds of revisions are included in the fee. Further revisions are invoiced as additional work.

5. Additional work
Changes or additions to the work shall be agreed in writing before work starts, and are invoiced at NOK 1,100 per hour excl. VAT.

6. Delivery
The deliverables shall be completed no later than 11 December 2026.

7. Acceptance
The delivery is considered accepted 5 days after delivery unless the Client has raised a written complaint before then.

8. Payment
The Supplier invoices on delivery. The Client shall pay the invoice no later than 14 days after the invoice date.

9. Intellectual property
All rights to material produced under this agreement, including sketches and rejected proposals, are transferred to the Client on signing of this agreement.

10. Liability
The Supplier's liability under this agreement shall not exceed the agreed fee.

11. Confidentiality
The parties shall keep information about each other's business confidential, also after the project has ended.

12. Governing law and disputes
This agreement is governed by Norwegian law. Disputes shall be resolved through negotiation, failing which Oslo District Court shall be the agreed venue.
