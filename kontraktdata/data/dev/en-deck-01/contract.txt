SERVICES AGREEMENT – Investor pitch deck for Polar Analytics

1. Parties
Client: Polar Analytics GmbH, reg. no. HRB 228734
Supplier: Kvist Creative AS, org. no. 927 330 214

2. Scope
This agreement covers the design of an investor pitch deck and a matching one-page summary. The work includes:
- Pitch deck of up to 20 slides
- Editable PowerPoint template
- One-page summary (PDF)
- Icon set of 15 icons
This agreement is based on the quote dated 02/11/2026.

3. Price
The agreed fee is NOK 45,000, exclusive of VAT.

4. Revisions
The fee covers up to 3 rounds of revisions. Revisions beyond this are invoiced as additional work.

5. Additional work
Work not covered by clause 2 is only carried out under a written agreement and invoiced on a time basis at NOK 1,100 per hour excl. VAT.

6. Delivery
Final delivery shall take place by 11/12/2026.

7. Acceptance
If the Client has not notified any defects in writing within 5 days of delivery, the delivery is deemed accepted.

8. Payment
The Supplier invoices on delivery. Invoices shall be paid within 14 days of the invoice date.

9. Intellectual property
The Client receives the rights to the final material when the full fee has been paid. The Supplier retains the rights to sketches and proposals that were not selected.

10. Liability
The Supplier's total liability is limited to the agreed fee.

11. Confidentiality
The parties shall keep information about each other's business confidential, also after the project has ended.

12. Governing law and disputes
This agreement is governed by Norwegian law. Disputes shall be resolved through negotiation, failing which Oslo District Court shall be the agreed venue.
