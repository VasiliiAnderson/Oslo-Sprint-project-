SERVICES AGREEMENT – Brand identity for Brightwater Outdoor

1. Parties
Client: Brightwater Outdoor Ltd, reg. no. UK 10482231
Supplier: Northlight Studio AS, org. no. 924 118 506

2. Scope
This agreement covers the design of a new brand identity for Brightwater's Nordic product line. The work includes:
- Logo in three versions
- Colour palette and typography
- Brand guidelines (PDF, approx. 20 pages)
- Packaging label template
This agreement is based on the quote dated 05/10/2026.

3. Price
The Client shall pay a fixed fee of NOK 92 000 incl. VAT.

4. Revisions
The fee covers up to 2 rounds of revisions. Revisions beyond this are invoiced as additional work.

5. Delivery
Final delivery shall take place by 22/01/2027.

6. Acceptance
The delivery is considered accepted 10 days after delivery unless the Client has raised a written complaint before then.

7. Payment
The Supplier invoices on delivery. Invoices shall be paid within 14 days of the invoice date.

8. Intellectual property
The Client receives the rights to the final material when the full fee has been paid. The Supplier retains the rights to sketches and proposals that were not selected.

9. Liability
The Supplier's total liability is limited to the agreed fee.

10. Confidentiality
The parties shall keep information about each other's business confidential, also after the project has ended.

11. Governing law and disputes
This agreement is governed by Norwegian law. Disputes shall be resolved through negotiation, failing which Oslo District Court shall be the agreed venue.

Place and date: Oslo, 19 October 2026
